## Commercial decisions awaiting evidence

- Decide whether free one-session use is evaluation-only or includes commercial
  production.
- Decide whether the free entitlement always receives the current Stable binary and
  identical core fingerprint/Humanize behavior, with paid tiers differentiated only by
  capacity and operational features.
- Validate the published 5, 20, 200, and 2,000 concurrent-session allowances and
  $19/$49/$199/$499 monthly promotional prices with qualified design partners.
- Do not activate Fleet or Grid self-serve checkout until concurrency enforcement,
  soak capacity, abuse controls, support cost, and margin evidence are complete.
- Define the browser-process counting rule, burst behavior, lease grace, cancellation,
  proration, and offline policy.
- Finalize legal entity, binary grant, privacy/telemetry disclosure, acceptable use,
  refund, support, governing-law, and OEM/SaaS terms.
- Confirm supported operating systems, release channels, response times, and Enterprise
  annual minimum.
- Decide whether and when a separately metered managed-cloud offer is economically and
  operationally justified.
